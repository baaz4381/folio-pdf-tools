Why it works this way

Files stay put

Every tool runs with JavaScript already loaded in your browser. Your PDF is never transmitted anywhere to be processed.

No accounts, no limits

There's nothing to sign up for and no daily cap. Open a tool, drop a file, download the result.

Works offline once loaded

After the page has loaded, most tools keep working even without a connection, since there's no server round-trip.
